The energy transition

Britain's last coal-fired power station closed in September 2024, ending nearly 150 years of coal generation. A carbon price floor and the shift to gas drove the change.

At the same time renewable generation rose steadily — the North Sea now hosts several of the world's largest offshore wind farms.

Data

Live figures come from Elexon (BMRS) and the Carbon Intensity API. Historical aggregates are served from an open backfill snapshot, rebuilt daily.
